Key figures: income peak

  • Age range of the salary peak: 42 to 60
  • Median salary at peak: 17.5 million
  • Median salary under 21: 13.5 million
  • Median requested age: 30.0 years

Interpretation of Table 13.1 and Figure 13.1: The median salary rises with the requested age, but the slope is gentle: from 9 million in the 24–26 band to 11 million in 27–35, 13.5 in 36–41 and 17.5 million in 42 and above. The “peak” is in the 42–60 band, but this band contains only 1,820 postings, and the 17.5 million figure reflects a few managerial roles rather than a general rule.

Two non-intuitive points: the 18–20 band has a median of 13.5 million (higher than 21–26 with 9 million); these postings are mainly specialised roles for young talent (for example sales or specialised internships) and their sample is small. Also, hourly income rises in the higher age bands, because the working hours of these roles are shorter.

Warning: the age band is the employer's preference. Using this table to set an “age cap” in a posting is both ethically problematic and, given the shortage of experienced staff, ultimately harmful to the employer.

industry_age_rows = []
for industry in ads.industry_group.value_counts().head(6).index:
    subset = paid.loc[paid.industry_group.eq(industry)]
    grouped = subset.groupby(pd.cut(subset.age_mid, bins=[17, 24, 29, 34, 39, 60],
                                    labels=['۱۸ تا ۲۴', '۲۵ تا ۲۹', '۳۰ تا ۳۴', '۳۵ تا ۳۹', '۴۰ و بالاتر']),
                            observed=True).agg(**{'تعداد': ('ad_id', 'size'),
                                                  'میانه حقوق': ('salary_mid', 'median')})
    grouped = grouped.loc[grouped['تعداد'].ge(MIN_N)]
    if grouped.empty:
        continue
    industry_age_rows.append({'صنعت': industry, 'تعداد آگهی': int(grouped['تعداد'].sum()),
                              'بازه اوج': grouped['میانه حقوق'].idxmax(),
                              'میانه اوج': grouped['میانه حقوق'].max(),
                              'میانه کم‌سن‌ترین بازه': grouped['میانه حقوق'].iloc[0]})
industry_age = pd.DataFrame(industry_age_rows).set_index('صنعت')

           sample=f'{int(paid.age_mid.notna().sum()):,} آگهی با حقوق و سن', index_name='صنعت',
           note='بازه‌های با کمتر از ۳۰ آگهی در تعیین اوج نادیده گرفته شده‌اند.')

Interpretation of Table 13.2 and Figure 13.2: The income peak differs across industries. In information technology and consumer goods, the peak is in the 40 and above band (+30% relative to the youngest band); in manufacturing, trade and “other industries” the peak is at 35–39; and in internet/e-commerce, the peak is in the very 18–24 band and the curve does not rise after that.

Interpretation: in knowledge-based industries, experience and age create added value up to the fourth decade of life; in internet and digital businesses, the market offers a higher price for a younger and cheaper workforce, and the age-growth path is flat. For a manager, this means the “age structure of the team” should be consistent with the business model, not with a single uniform rule.
